Our story

From a small carpentry shop to a full furniture studio

We began by making doors, cots and cupboards for neighbours. Word spread, orders grew, and today we design and build complete bedrooms, living rooms and kitchens for homes across Maharashtra.

What hasn’t changed is how we work: measure properly, talk honestly, build carefully, and stay until the last cushion is in place.

  • Own workshop with carpenters, upholsterers and polishers
  • Direct from maker to home — no middlemen
  • Support that continues long after delivery
