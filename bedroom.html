Buying guide

Choosing the right bed

A good bed is part furniture, part architecture. A few quick checks before you decide:

  • Leave at least 60 cm walking space on both sides
  • Headboard height: 110–130 cm suits most standard ceilings
  • Storage beds add 15–20 cm of height — plan side tables to match
  • Ask for a fabric swatch kit before choosing upholstery
