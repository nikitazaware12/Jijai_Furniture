Buying guide

Getting the table size right

Plan about 60 cm of table width per person, and leave 90 cm of space around the table for chairs.

  • Round tables suit 4–5 people in smaller rooms
  • Rectangular tables work best for 6+ seats
  • Coffee table height: just below your sofa seat
  • Marble and wood are both finished with stain-resistant sealers
