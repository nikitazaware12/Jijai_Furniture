Royal & Classic

Carved, gilded, unforgettable

Hand-carved frames, gold leaf, velvet and brocade. Royal pieces take longer — each frame is carved and finished by hand — but they become heirlooms.

  • Solid teak and sheesham frames with hand-carved relief
  • Gold-leaf, bronze and antique polish finishes
  • Velvet, silk and brocade upholstery
  • Best with high ceilings, chandeliers and warm lighting

Modern Minimal

Clean lines, quiet confidence

Slim silhouettes, matte finishes and a calm palette. Modern pieces make smaller rooms feel larger and are easy to live with every day.

  • Slim metal or timber legs that keep floors visible
  • Charcoal, white, grey and walnut palettes
  • Storage hidden inside sofas, beds and tables
  • Perfect for apartments and open-plan layouts

Soft Contemporary

Curves, texture and warmth

Rounded shapes, bouclé, rattan and gentle earthy colours. This is the style behind the cosy, photograph-worthy homes everyone is saving to their boards.

  • Sculpted headboards and curved sofas
  • Bouclé, textured weaves and ribbed panels
  • Sage, cream, taupe and terracotta tones
  • Great mixed with natural wood and brass
