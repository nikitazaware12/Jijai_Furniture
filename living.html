Buying guide

How to size a sofa

The most common mistake is buying a sofa that overwhelms the room — or one that floats awkwardly in it.

  • Leave 45 cm between sofa and coffee table
  • Leave 75–90 cm for walkways
  • Pick performance fabrics if you have kids or pets
  • Rounded corners work well in compact rooms
