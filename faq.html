Ordering & custom work

Can I change the size or colour of any piece?

Yes. Every design on this site is made to order, so we can adjust dimensions, fabric, wood finish and hardware without an extra “customisation fee”.

Do you take completely new designs?

Absolutely. Share a reference photo or sketch and we’ll prepare a 3D preview and a fixed quote before you commit.

How do I place an order?

Request a quote, approve the design and pay a 50% advance. The balance is due before delivery.

Timelines & delivery

How long does an order take?

Most sofas and tables take 3–5 weeks, beds 4–6 weeks, kitchens 5–7 weeks and carved royal pieces 8–12 weeks. You get a firm date with your quote.

Do you deliver outside Pune?

We deliver across Maharashtra with our own vehicles and can arrange transport to other states. Delivery charges are shown in your quote.

Is assembly included?

Yes. Our team assembles everything at your home and removes all packaging.

Materials & care

What wood do you use?

Seasoned teak, sheesham and oak for solid pieces; BWP plywood and engineered boards for kitchens and wardrobes. We can show samples at the showroom.

How do I care for upholstery?

Vacuum gently each week, blot spills quickly and avoid direct sunlight. We provide a care card with every piece and offer fabric-protection treatments.

Do your pieces come with a warranty?

Frames carry a 5-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Fabric and hardware carry 1 year. Wear and misuse are not covered.
